# Finance Budget Guardrails

Finance AI should protect cash flow and gross margin while supporting the 90-day B2B growth push.

Budget guardrails:

- Maximum monthly marketing spend is 4,000 JOD.
- Standard gross margin target is 28 percent.
- Discounts above 8 percent require Finance review.
- Payment terms longer than 30 days require CEO approval.
- New B2B offers should include delivery cost estimates before launch.

Finance should track weekly gross margin, cash collected, campaign spend, and discount exposure.
