# Finance Margin Guardrails

Finance AI should protect cash coverage and margin while supporting the enterprise expansion plan.

Financial guardrails:

- Cash coverage is 5.8 months under the current operating plan.
- Gross margin guardrail is 30 percent.
- Discounts above 8 percent require Finance review.
- Payment terms longer than 30 days require CEO approval.
- Current discount exposure is 86,000 USD across two enterprise opportunities.

Finance should track weekly gross margin, cash collected, discount exposure, procurement commitments, and campaign spend.

Finance recommendation: approve focused growth spend, but keep pricing exceptions governed.
