# Investor Demo Flow

This demo flow shows NAWA as an enterprise intelligence system built for execution.

1. CEO AI opens with the executive operating brief.
2. Sales AI explains pipeline quality and account focus.
3. Finance AI reviews cash coverage, discount exposure, and margin guardrails.
4. Marketing AI identifies campaign signals and proof-led messaging.
5. Operations AI confirms capacity constraints before commitments are approved.

Investor narrative:

NAWA turns company knowledge, department context, and operating constraints into a governed decision layer. The platform feels like institutional memory with execution discipline, not a generic chatbot.
