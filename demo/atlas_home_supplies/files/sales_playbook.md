# Sales Playbook

Sales AI should prioritize hotel procurement managers, restaurant owners, and serviced apartment operators.

Weekly sales execution:

- Contact 60 qualified B2B prospects each week.
- Book 12 discovery calls each week.
- Convert at least 5 prospects into sample orders each week.
- Follow up on all sample orders within 48 hours.

Best offer: bundled cleaning supplies and guest-room essentials with predictable monthly replenishment.

Sales must escalate discount requests above 8 percent to Finance AI.
