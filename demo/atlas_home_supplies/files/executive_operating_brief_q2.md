# Executive Operating Brief Q2

Northstar is ready to accelerate, but the next phase should be governed by fulfillment capacity, margin protection, and account-level focus.

Executive summary:

- Revenue quality is improving, with the strongest expansion signal in repeat enterprise accounts.
- Execution risk has shifted from demand generation to fulfillment capacity.
- Finance can support the growth plan if discounts and payment terms remain governed.
- Marketing should lead with operational reliability and measurable execution proof.

Recommended CEO decision:

Approve the 14-account expansion plan, require Finance review for discounts above 8 percent, and ask Operations for a weekly capacity checkpoint before delivery-heavy proposals are committed.
