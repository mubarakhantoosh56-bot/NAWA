# Company Brain Architecture

NAWA is organized around a Company Brain layer.

## Inputs

NAWA receives signals from:

- Chat and manual notes
- Forms and operational updates
- Files and images
- ERP systems
- HR systems
- Accounting systems
- Attendance systems
- Sales systems
- Warehouse systems
- Future n8n automations

## Intelligence Layers

The core layers are:

1. Raw input capture
2. Entity extraction
3. Classification
4. Structured record drafts
5. Operational events
6. Pattern detection
7. Decision context
8. Organizational intelligence
9. AI insight and generated outputs

## Company Brain Responsibilities

NAWA should understand:

- Company structure
- Divisions
- Departments
- Users and roles
- Operational units
- Workflows
- Department dependencies
- KPI ownership
- Files and knowledge
- HR and attendance signals
- Operational events and patterns

The architecture must keep multi-tenant isolation, auth, role visibility, bilingual support, operational memory, decision context, and pattern detection intact.
