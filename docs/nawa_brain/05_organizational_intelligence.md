# Organizational Intelligence

NAWA must understand the company as a connected operating organization.

## Required Entities

NAWA should model and reason about:

- Company
- Divisions
- Departments
- Users
- Roles
- Operational units
- Workflows
- Dependencies
- KPI ownership
- Operational relationships

## Department Dependencies

Examples:

- Sales depends on production, warehouse, and logistics.
- Production depends on procurement, inventory, staffing, and HR coverage.
- HR affects operational execution through attendance, leave, shifts, and performance.
- Finance affects procurement, supplier reliability, working capital, and scaling.
- Warehouse affects Sales promises and Production planning.

## User Intelligence

Users are operational entities. A user may have:

- Role
- Department
- Attendance
- Leave records
- Activity history
- Operational impact
- KPI ownership
- Related workflows
- Issues and history

## HR Operational Layer

NAWA should support:

- Attendance
- Absence
- Leave
- Shifts
- Performance notes
- Productivity signals

HR signals must be treated as operational execution signals, not isolated HR records.
