# Jannat Al-Firdaws Enterprise Model

Jannat Al-Firdaws is the first real enterprise environment for NAWA.

## Structure

```text
Jannat Al-Firdaws
├── Dairtna Poultry
├── Caesar Beverage
└── Shared Corporate Departments
```

## Dairtna Poultry

NAWA should understand Dairtna Poultry through:

- Halls
- Feed signals
- Egg production signals
- Veterinary signals
- Mortality and health issues
- Warehouse and inventory
- Sales and distribution
- Finance and procurement impact
- HR and staffing impact

## Caesar Beverage

NAWA should understand Caesar Beverage through:

- Production signals
- Warehouse signals
- Sales and distribution
- Marketing signals
- Finance signals
- Inventory and fulfillment readiness

## Shared Corporate Departments

Shared Corporate includes:

- HR
- Procurement
- Corporate Finance
- Accounting
- Assets
- Leadership and governance

These shared departments are not back-office decoration. They directly affect operating execution across Dairtna Poultry and Caesar Beverage.
