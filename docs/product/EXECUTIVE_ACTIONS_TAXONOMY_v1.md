# 1. Purpose

This document defines how NAWA generates and presents Executive Actions.

Executive Actions are management decisions recommended by NAWA based on validated operational intelligence.

They are not additional data requests.

---

# 2. Philosophy

An Executive Brief without Executive Actions is incomplete.

Understanding the situation is valuable.

Knowing what to do next is decisive.

Executive Actions transform intelligence into execution.

---

# 3. Definition

Executive Actions represent recommended management actions that help reduce business risk, improve operational performance, or prevent future issues.

Every action should support executive decision making.

---

# 4. Action Categories

Executive Actions may belong to one or more categories.

- Immediate Action
- Investigation
- Monitoring
- Escalation
- Resource Allocation
- Operational Improvement
- Strategic Decision
- Preventive Action
- Corrective Action

---

# 5. Priority Levels

Every Executive Action must include a priority.

- Critical
- High
- Medium
- Low

Priority must always be justified.

---

# 6. Action Rules

Every Executive Action should explain:

- What should be done?
- Why should it be done?
- Expected business outcome.
- Supporting evidence.
- Priority.

---

# 7. Relationship with Company Inputs

Executive Actions and Recommended Company Inputs are independent concepts.

Executive Actions

→ Actions management should perform.

Recommended Company Inputs

→ Additional information NAWA requires to improve confidence.

These sections must never be merged.

---

# 8. Evidence Rules

Executive Actions must never be generated without supporting evidence.

If confidence is reduced:

- explain why,
- identify missing evidence,
- recommend additional Company Inputs when appropriate.

---

# 9. Executive Language

Executive Actions should be:

- Clear
- Specific
- Actionable
- Business-focused
- Easy to execute

Avoid technical implementation language.

---

# 10. Never Do

Executive Actions must never:

- Recommend unsupported actions.
- Repeat operational facts.
- Replace Business Impact.
- Replace Executive Assessment.
- Ask for Company Inputs as if they were Executive Actions.

---

# 11. Future Expansion

This taxonomy applies across every NAWA business domain and will evolve as additional Executive Decision Support capabilities are introduced.

---

# 12. Guiding Statement

Executive intelligence creates understanding.

Executive Actions create results.
