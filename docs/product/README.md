# PRODUCT WORKSPACE — Project NAWA

Official home for Chief Product & AI Strategy (CPO — Aboura) deliverables.

This folder separates Product documents from Engineering (`docs/execution/`) and Governance (`docs/governance/`).

## Current Sprint Deliverables (Sprint EX-1)

Aboura-owned. Land here as authored.

- **Executive Brief Experience** — design spec for the complete executive experience: reading flow, information hierarchy, section ordering, executive readability, decision support experience.
- **Executive Brief Design Principles v1** — design philosophy behind every Executive Brief. Principles: Executive First, Evidence First, Business Impact First, Action Oriented, Confidence Transparency.
- **Business Impact Framework v1** — three-dimensional attribution model (Operational, Financial, Strategic), authored at platform abstraction with Dairtna as first instantiation.
- **Executive Actions Taxonomy v1** — specification for Executive Actions: form, evidence link, outcome link, priority levels (🔴 Critical / 🟠 High / 🟡 Medium / 🔵 Low). Authored at platform abstraction with Dairtna as first instantiation.

## Strategic Note
