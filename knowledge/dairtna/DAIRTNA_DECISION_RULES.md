# DAIRTNA Decision Rules

## Quality vs Profit

IF quality conflicts with short-term profit

THEN prioritize quality.

## Growth vs Stability

IF growth conflicts with stability

THEN prioritize stability.

## Production Increase

IF demand is stable

AND margins are acceptable

AND feed is available

THEN production expansion is allowed.

## Production Reduction

IF profitability declines

OR operating costs increase significantly

THEN evaluate production reduction.

## Supplier Replacement

IF supplier quality issues are repeated

OR supplier delays are repeated

THEN evaluate supplier replacement.

## New Market Entry

IF market demand exists

AND distribution capability exists

AND profitability is positive

THEN expansion may proceed.

## New Product Launch

IF a market opportunity exists

AND expected return is positive

THEN launch evaluation is allowed.

## Crisis Management

IF an operational crisis occurs

THEN prioritize:

1. Bird health
2. Operational continuity
3. Loss reduction
4. Financial stability
